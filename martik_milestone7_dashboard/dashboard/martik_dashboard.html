Largest unpaid balances

Customer groups (RFM)

Gone quiet: call these first

Quiet means no order for more than three times the account's usual gap between orders, for 30 to 150 days. Longer than that is a lapsed account. Schools go quiet in school holidays, so check the term calendar before calling. The group "At risk" in the table on the left is a relative score: most accounts order almost daily, so some At-risk accounts are only days behind.

What this means for the store

Findings from Milestones 3 to 6, for the whole period Oct 2024 to Sep 2026. Each one can be checked in the matching milestone folder.

Stock up for October to December

Peak weeks earn about 1.76 times low-season weeks (interval 1.67 to 1.86). December 2025 was the best month at KES 5.16 million, and January falls about 50% from December every year.

Plan stock, cash and delivery staff for the peak by the end of September.

A few accounts carry the business

The largest account (C001 Coffee Chain) is 16.9% of account revenue and the top seven accounts are 52.7%. Hotels (24%) and restaurants (23%) are the biggest segments.

Protect these relationships; losing one hotel would be felt in the weekly totals.

Fruit leads in town, fries lead in Ruiru

CBD fruit is 20.4% of revenue. Ruiru potato products are 16.7% at a 30.2% margin. Cucumber, matoke, white onion, lemon and coriander are top sellers in their category but earn less margin than the category average.

Review the price of those five lines first.

Credit is the biggest risk

KES 3.95 million is unpaid, of which KES 852,000 is overdue across 147 invoices, and KES 56,000 is written off. Hotels and schools pay after the due date 99% of the time, about 14 to 16 days late. Seven accounts owe more than their credit limit, the worst being C101 Restaurant (169%), C150 School (163%) and C111 Restaurant (142%).

Chase hotels and schools first, and stop new credit to accounts over their limit.

Four active accounts have gone quiet

16 Champions bring 55% of revenue. Four accounts that used to order every few days have not ordered for 83 to 145 days: C133 Hotel (KES 6.0M to date), C105 Restaurant, C145 Caterer and C135 Hotel. Another 14 have been quiet longer and are probably lost.

Call the four accounts in the Gone quiet table this week.

M-Pesa does not change how much customers order

M-Pesa orders look 38% smaller, but only because small shops use M-Pesa and hotels use bank transfer. The same customer orders the same amount either way.

Offer M-Pesa for convenience; do not expect bigger orders from it.

Discounts: only January fruit looks useful

The two January fruit promotions raised fruit sales by about 17 to 23%, but no promotion passes a strict test. The other three cannot be called a success or a failure.

Next time, run a promotion on a random half of accounts so the other half is a fair comparison.

Trust the forecast, but not blindly in the peak

On test weeks the forecast is off by about 12% (WAPE), against 17% for a 4-week average. In the Oct 2025 peak the model alone under-forecast by 14%, so the page shows a blend with last year's pattern.

Plan for the forecast and be ready for the top of its 80% range.
